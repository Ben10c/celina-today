Tuesday - 20 minutes

ESB pre-learning: one slide for the Curriculum Talk

Your role: INTERVIEWER. Ask, don't tell. She chooses and places every image, and you ask why she picked each one.

Open the Google Classroom template assignment 'My ESB Curriculum Talk – My animal'. Use the same rainforest or savanna animal as last week's notes. The WAGOLL example is a sloth, so look at it together first. Images can come from Encyclopedia Britannica.

1. In its habitat

The animal in its home. Ask: "Which photo shows it in the wild best?"

2. The habitat

Rainforest or savanna on its own. Ask: "What would you see, hear and feel there?"

3. Close-up

Appearance or an adaptation. Ask: "What part of its body helps it survive?"

4. What it eats

Its food. Ask: "Is it a herbivore, carnivore or omnivore? How does it get its food?"

Finish line: one slide with four images, one in each box, saved on Classroom. Each picture should match a part of last week's notes.

Optional stretch — Yohaku

Yohaku puzzles give a grid with row and column totals (sums or products). She fills in the missing numbers so every line hits its target. Let her try first, and only give the rule if she's stuck for more than a couple of minutes.

Chinese — printed tasks, 20 min

Do the printed Chinese home learning sheet after a short break. Ask her to read the instructions aloud and explain them before she starts. Leave the answers to her.

Friday - 20 minutes

Spelling Unit 4: suffix -ly

Your role: QUIZMASTER. Once she has written the list, read the words aloud while she writes. Mark together and re-test the misses.

Write the Vocab Vault words in alphabetical order, three times each, in Book B.

Answer key (A–Z)

  1. busily
  2. frantically
  3. happily
  4. heroically
  5. magically
  6. naughtily
  7. physically
  8. sleepily

Spelling patterns

y → i + ly: busy → busily, happy → happily, naughty → naughtily, sleepy → sleepily.

-ic → -ically: frantic → frantically, heroic → heroically, magic → magically.

-al + ly: physical → physically.

ABC tricky bit

The h words: hap- comes before her- because a comes before e.

Optional stretch — Suffix Sprint

Follow the sheet. A quick version: give her a root word (quick, angry, gentle, lazy) and she writes the -ly form against the clock.

Finish line: 8 words × 3 in A–Z order, then a spoken test. It's Deepavali day, so stop at 20 minutes.
