Tuesday - 20 minutes

ESB: choose one poem

Your role: present the two choices and help her settle on one. Celina chooses the poem and owns the copy.

ESB stands for English Speaking Board. This week's pre-learning starts the "speak by heart" strand. For tonight, she has two prepared choices:

  • The Owl and the Pussy-Cat — Edward Lear — funny adventure story with memorable characters and repetition.
  • Us Two — A. A. Milne — warm, playful friendship poem about facing adventures together.

Thursday - 20 minutes

Maths: division

Your role: protect the method and the time. She chooses one challenge and works in the squared-paper home learning book.

This is Year 3 division practice. Remember that a remainder is what is left after making equal groups, and it must be smaller than the number being divided by. She may draw equal groups or use a number line, and should write remainders using r.

Optional stretch

Divide 26, 27, 28, 29 and 30 by 4, notice the remainder pattern, then predict the remainders for 31, 36 and 47. Explain why a remainder of 4 is impossible when dividing by 4.

Friday - 20 minutes

Spelling: -sure and -ture

Your role: help her recall the pattern, then let her explain and check the words herself.

Recap -sure with the /zhuh/ sound, as in pleasure, and the -ture ending, as in adventure. Choose words from the Vocab Vault and apply the pattern rather than trying to copy every activity.

  • pressure
  • closure
  • treasure
  • leisure
  • pleasure
  • measure
  • composure
  • disclosure
  • enclosure
  • exposure
  • mixture
  • picture
  • puncture
  • creature
  • adventure
  • capture

Optional stretch

Choose one word, find two or three synonyms in a dictionary, and decide whether each synonym sounds positive, neutral or negative. For a visual check, write the consonants in one colour, vowels in another, and add syllable slices such as en / clo / sure.
