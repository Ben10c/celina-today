Tuesday - 20 minutes

Science: Rainforest Top Trumps

Your role: keep time and ask for the adaptation explanation. Celina does the choosing, drawing and writing.

Choose at least three real animals that live in the tropical rainforest. For each card, write the animal's name, draw it carefully, score each feature from 1 to 5, and add one special feature explaining how it helps the animal survive.

0-3 minChoose three animals
3-15 minDraw and score
15-20 minExplain one adaptation

Suggested score headings

  • Speed - how quickly can it move?
  • Climbing - how well can it move through trees?
  • Camouflage - how well can it hide?
  • Defence - how well can it protect itself?

Land the idea: an adaptation is a body-part feature or behaviour that helps an animal survive in its habitat. Ask, "What does this feature help it do?" rather than accepting only a description.

The school WAGOLL models a jaguar, three-toed sloth and poison dart frog. Those are examples to understand, not a requirement to copy.

Thursday - 20 minutes

Maths: multiplication

Your role: protect the method and the time. She chooses one challenge and works in the squared-paper home learning book.

This is Year 3 multiplication practice. Use a part-whole model: multiply the tens and ones separately, add the partial products, and exchange when needed. Show the working and stop at 20 minutes.

Optional stretch

Use three of the digit cards 2, 3, 5, 6 and 8 once each to make a two-digit x one-digit multiplication. Find the largest and smallest products, an arrangement requiring exchange in both columns, and two different products between 250 and 450.

Friday - 20 minutes

Spelling: -sure and -ture

Your role: dictate or point to the word stems; let her recall the ending and check it herself.

Recap -sure with the /zhuh/ sound, as in pleasure, and the -ture ending, as in picture. Complete the missing endings, then choose one word for the optional word web.

  • pressure
  • closure
  • treasure
  • leisure
  • pleasure
  • measure
  • mixture
  • picture
  • puncture
  • creature
  • adventure
  • capture

Optional stretch

Pick one spelling word and make a word web with synonyms, antonyms, related words and an example sentence. For example, treasure could connect to jewels, gold, cash, gems, ruby, diamond, hoard, stash and cherish.
