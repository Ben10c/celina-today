Tuesday - 20 minutes

Pre-learning: diversity in our community

Your role: ask questions and let Celina decide what represents her family, interests and community.

After watching the assigned video, discuss why diversity in the classroom and school matters and is worth celebrating. Celina should jot down or draw her ideas in Book A.

Culture or heritage

Choose something connected to family background, language, food or celebrations.

Interests

Choose something that shows what she loves or how her mind works.

Community connection

Choose something that connects her to other people, such as sport, the neighbourhood or friendship.

Finish line: select three small, non-precious items, put them in a sandwich bag, and bring them to school for the real diversity jar.

Thursday - 20 minutes

Maths: times-table recall

Your role: protect the 20-minute limit. Celina completes one activity on the squared paper in Book A with her best presentation.

The three choices mix multiplication and related division facts. Use the activity selected by her teacher; if none was specified, let Celina choose the level that gives useful practice without turning it into a speed test.

0-2 minChoose one activity
2-18 minRecall and record
18-20 minCheck related facts

Optional stretch

Try a Yohaku multiplication puzzle: fill four cells so the two numbers in each row and each column multiply to the printed totals. The sum of all four cells is also given as a check.

Friday - 20 minutes

ESB: choose a book extract

Your role: help narrow the book choice and time the reading. Celina should make the final choice and explain it in her own words.

  • Choose a section of four or five pages.
  • Make sure it contains dialogue.
  • Practise reading it aloud with expression.
  • Identify the character she would most like to meet and explain why.

In Book A, use the subheading Task 2: ESB book, then record the book title, author and page numbers, followed by “I would most like to meet...” and her reason.

Finish line: mark the chosen pages and bring the physical book to school on Monday so the extract can be copied.
