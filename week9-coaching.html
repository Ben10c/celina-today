Tuesday - 25 minutes

ESB: start animal notes (Curriculum Talk)

Your role: INTERVIEWER. Ask, don't tell. Help her choose an animal she can talk about with interest, then let her own the notes.

Curriculum Talk is 40% of ESB. She chooses one animal from a rainforest or savanna biome. Speech writing is in class later — tonight is notes only, on the printed template.

Choose

Rainforest or savanna animal she can research. Avoid something so obscure that sources are thin.

Introduction

What is the animal? Why this one? One or two clear sentences she can later turn into a hook.

Appearance

Size, colour, distinctive features. Bullet notes are fine — this is not the speech yet.

Finish line: animal chosen; Introduction and Appearance filled on the template. Habitat and Diet wait for Friday.

Optional stretch — The Changing Array

Mo starts with 7 rows of 8 (56). He removes one row but adds one counter to every remaining row → 6 rows of 9 (54). There are enough dots from the removed row (8), with 2 left over. Facts: 7×8=56 and 6×9=54; the first array has more. Same idea with 8×9 → 7×10: leftover 2 again. Challenge: another pair (e.g. 5×6 → 4×7). The difference stays 2 because n(n+1) − (n−1)(n+2) = 2.
